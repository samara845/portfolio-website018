about

Development, AI and marketing — under one roof

I'm a BCA student at Veer Kunwar Singh University, Ara, Bihar, and a technology-focused developer working at the intersection of web development, AI and digital marketing.

Most developers stop at code. Most marketers never touch a codebase. I sit in between — building the site, wiring in AI where it genuinely helps, and making sure people can actually find and trust it once it's live.

Through WebzeaAi, my technology initiative, I help businesses, freelancers and fellow students turn websites, AI tools and digital marketing into practical, working solutions — not just checkboxes.

I also teach what I build. Alongside client work, I train students in practical development skills and how to use AI tools effectively in real projects.
